## Quick Reference

### NodeMCU Default Network
- **SSID**: `GhostH`
- **Password**: `ghosth1234`
- **Web Portal**: `http://192.168.4.1`
- **TCP Telemetry Stream**: `192.168.4.1:8888`
- **Serial Baud Rate**: `115200` (8N1)

### Sensor Pinout
- **GPIO 4 (`D2`)**: LM393 Optical IR Detector Input
- **GPIO 5 (`D1`)**: Buzzer Alert Output
- **A0**: Raw IR Amplitude Input
